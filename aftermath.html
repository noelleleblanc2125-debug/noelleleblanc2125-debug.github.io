The Aftermath & Transition of Things Falling Apart explores what happens after the expectations we once held begin to shift. This phase focuses on the uncertainty of the in-between: navigating changing relationships, reconsidering familiar timelines, comparing our progress to others, and figuring out what comes next when life no longer looks exactly the way we imagined it would.
