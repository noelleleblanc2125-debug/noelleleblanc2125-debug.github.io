Feelings of Being "Behind"

☆

Posts about feeling behind expected timelines, comparing our progress to others, and questioning where we thought we were supposed to be by now.

♡

I like how this screenshot embraces the idea that even as time continues to go by, we do not automatically figure things out. People say, "it comes with time," but this screenshot suggests that not everything does. Some things come with a change of mindset.

This screenshot speaks to an experience that I had and a lot of people around me seemed to be having. As I got older, I thought there was this set timeline. I go to college. I get my degree. And then within a couple of months, I would have a job. But I did not have an "adult job" or a job I could turn into a career until a year after I graduated with my undergrad degree.

When you think about it this way, maybe young adults who are entering the job market aren't "behind," this screenshot would suggest they just have their priorities straight.

This perspective makes me re-examone my own situation and whether I am "behind" or I just haven't been offered an opportunity that aligns with me.
